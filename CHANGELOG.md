# What's new in Stevie Clawdberg

The changes you'd notice in the Stevie Clawdberg universe, newest first. The full, detailed history (every fix, every version) lives in [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash).

## 2026-10-17

- **The trailer plays for everyone.** The video at the top of this page only played for people signed in to GitHub. It's uploaded again.

## Clawd Clash 3.4.0 (2026-10-16)

- **A new universe.** Stevie Clawdberg joins Star Clawds, Marvelous Clawds, Detective Clawds, Doctor Clawd and The Clawd Files: pick it in **Settings → Universe**, or in the screen saver's Options.
- **117 characters from nine films.** Indiana Bones knocking his own hat off with his whip, Marty McFry fighting with his guitar, Doc Brawn in his brainwave analyser steering a remote-controlled DeLorean, E.Clawd and his glowing finger, the Goonies (Chunk's Truffle Shuffle, Data's Pinchers of Peril), Jurassic Park's people (Muldune's *"Clever girl…"*), the Jaws crew, the Close Encounters family, Peter Panning and the Lost Boys. Facing them, Bellock and Mola Ramm, Biff in every decade (Mad Dog Tannin's *"Dance! Come on, runt!"*), the gremlins, Bruise the Shark, the Fratellys, T. Wrecks and the raptors, Captain Clawk and his crew. HEM turns up in a fedora with a bullwhip.
- **23 places**, from Hill Valley in 1955, 1885 and Biff's Pleasure Paradise to Amity Island, Kingston Falls, the moonlit hill, the Goondocks, the idol temple, the park gates towering over the wall, Devils Tower, Neverland and the Jolly Roger at anchor, each with something moving in it.
- **Its own rides.** The DeLorean picks up Back to the Future's people and leaves two lines of fire; the E.T. kids and the Goonies fly off on the moon bike; Close Encounters' people walk up into the mothership's light; Jurassic Park's people leave in the park jeep.
- **88 mph.** When a Back to the Future time traveller's session compacts its context, blue sparks crackle, a white flash and they're gone in two lines of fire, then three sonic booms bring them back as OUTATIME types itself out over their head.
- **Indy shoots first.** The Cairo Swordsman flashes his sword; Indy draws his revolver.
- **Ten transitions** from the films: the bike crossing the moon, 88 mph, the map route, the fin, a flashbulb, the park gates, the five tones, the rolling boulder, pixie dust and One-Eyed Willy's doubloon.
- **Habits of its own**: a glass of water ripples in front of T. Wrecks with every step, and Captain Clawk jumps at every tick of the Croc Clock.
- **Arch-enemies face off first** (Indy and Bellock, Marty and Mad Dog, Doc and Old Biff, Billy and Strype…).
- **Clawd Royale**: Stevie Clawdberg's cast joins everyone else's in one scene when you pick Clawd Royale.
- **A trailer of its own**, at the top of this page.
